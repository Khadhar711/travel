About Regional Cuisine

Andhra Pradesh is renowned for its spicy and flavorful cuisine. Signature dishes include biryani, gongura pachadi, pulusu, and a variety of pickles. Meals are often served on banana leaves and are known for their bold use of chilies and tamarind. Each region, from Coastal Andhra to Rayalaseema, adds its own twist to the culinary landscape.
