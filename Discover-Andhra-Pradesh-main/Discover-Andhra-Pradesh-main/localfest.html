About Local Festivals

Andhra Pradesh is home to diverse festivals such as Ugadi (Telugu New Year), Sankranti, Dussehra, and the Tirupati Brahmotsavam. Each celebration showcases the state’s deep-rooted customs, spirituality, and artistic flair. These festivals are marked by rituals, dances, music, and traditional cuisine that bring communities together.
