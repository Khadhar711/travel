About Traditional Crafts

Andhra Pradesh is known for its exquisite traditional crafts like Etikoppaka wooden toys, Kondapalli dolls, Bidriware, and Kalamkari paintings. These crafts not only reflect artistic brilliance but also tell stories of cultural significance. The use of natural dyes, intricate carvings, and sustainable materials makes these items unique and cherished globally.
